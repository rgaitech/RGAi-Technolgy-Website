Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common inquiries before you initiate a project brief.

What is your typical project estimation model? ▼
We provide custom deliverables-based estimation. After you submit a project brief, we will schedule a 30-minute discovery call to scope your features and supply a granular estimation plan with transparent milestones.
Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)? ▼
Yes, always. We prioritize intellectual property protection. We sign standard corporate NDAs before discussing any proprietary specifications, pipeline logs, or company operations.
What is the average timeline for an AI or App project? ▼
Timeline varies depending on technical complexity. Basic website designs can deploy in 2–4 weeks. Complex enterprise tools, machine learning model fine-tuning, or mobile app modules typically require 2–4 months from discovery to launch.
What is your core tech stack? ▼
Our web solutions are built using modern React, Next.js, and Node.js infrastructures. For mobile apps, we deploy cross-platform React Native and Flutter. Our AI automation engines run on Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, and unified vector databases.
Do you offer post-launch maintenance and support? ▼
Absolutely. Every project launch includes a complimentary 30-day warrantee support cycle. We also provide ongoing SLA retainer plans for hosting optimization, model retraining, API scaling, and routine software revisions.
